Banana Bread

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a standard 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas in a large mixing bowl using a fork until completely smooth.
  3. Stir the melted butter thoroughly into the mashed bananas.
  4. Mix in the baking soda and salt.
  5. Blend in the brown sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract until the mixture is uniform.
  6. Gently stir in the flour just until the dry pockets disappear—be careful not to overmix, or the bread will become dense.
  7. Fold in your mix-ins (like walnuts or chocolate chips) if you are using them.
  8. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan.
  9. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the center; it should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  10. Cool completely by letting the loaf rest in the pan for 10 minutes, then transferring it onto a wire rack to slice.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 ⅓ cups mashed overripe bananas

Blueberry Muffin

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a muffin tray with liners.
  2. Whisk together the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a large bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the oil, egg, milk, and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Fold the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients just until combined.
  5. Gently stir in the blueberries without overmixing.
  6. Divide the batter evenly into the muffin cups and bake for 18 to 22 minutes.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ⅓ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg
  • ⅓ - ½ cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries

Cinnamon Rolls

Directions

  1. Roll out the thawed bread dough into a rectangular shape.
  2. Brush with melted butter and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar and walnuts.
  3. Roll the dough tightly, slice it into rolls, and place them in a baking dish.
  4. Pour in the cream and let the rolls rise until puffy.
  5. Bake until golden brown, then drizzle with the glaze made from confectioners' sugar, milk, and vanilla.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 1 (1 pound) loaf frozen bread dough, thawed
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • ⅔ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon water, or as needed
  • ⅓ cup heavy whipping cream
  • ⅔ cup sifted confectioners' sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1 dash vanilla extract

Chocolate Cake

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven and grease your cake pans.
  2. Combine the boiling water and cocoa powder to make a smooth paste.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar together until fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well, then stir in the vanilla.
  5. Mix in the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  6. Fold in the cocoa mixture and pour the batter into the pans.
  7. Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 2 cups boiling water
  • 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 2 ¼ cups white sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 ½ teaspoons vanilla extract

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet.
  2. Cream the butter with the granulated and brown sugars until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the vanilla and eggs.
  4. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop the dough onto the tray and bake for 9 to 11 minutes.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups (12-oz. pkg.) semi-sweet chocolate chips

Elephant Ears

Directions

  1. Combine the flour, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Add ice-cold water gradually until the dough comes together.
  4. Roll the dough thinly and cut into large circles or strips.
  5. Fry until puffed and golden, then sprinkle generously with sugar.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/8 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 ¼ cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 stick (½ cup / 115 g) unsalted butter, very cold and cut into small pieces
  • 3 to 5 tbsp ice-cold water

Glazed Donut

Directions

  1. Activate the yeast in warm milk and let it sit until foamy.
  2. Mix in the sugar, eggs, butter, salt, and flour to form a dough.
  3. Knead the dough until smooth, then let it rise until doubled.
  4. Roll out the dough, cut into donut shapes, and fry until golden brown.
  5. Whisk the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla to make the glaze, then dip the donuts.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 4½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm whole milk
  • 2½ teaspoons active dry yeast (or instant yeast)
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • Vegetable or peanut oil (for frying)
  • 3 cups powdered sugar
  • 6 tablespoons milk
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

Plantain Tarts

Directions

  1. Rub the cold butter and shortening into the flour, sugar, and salt.
  2. Add cold water a little at a time until the dough just comes together.
  3. Chill the dough, then roll it out and cut into tart shells.
  4. Fill with your plantain mixture and seal the edges.
  5. Bake until the crust is golden and crisp.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cold butter (cubed)
  • ½ cup cold vegetable shortening
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 4–6 tbsp ice-cold water

Twisted Donut

Directions

  1. Activate the yeast in warm milk until bubbly.
  2. Mix the dough ingredients together and knead until smooth.
  3. Let the dough rise until doubled in size.
  4. Roll the dough, cut into strips, twist each strip, and fry until golden.
  5. Glaze with the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla mixture.

Ingredients needed for this recipe:

  • 4½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm whole milk
  • 2½ teaspoons active dry yeast (or instant yeast)
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • Vegetable or peanut oil (for frying)
  • 3 cups powdered sugar
  • 6 tablespoons milk
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
